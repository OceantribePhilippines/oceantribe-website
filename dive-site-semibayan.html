What you'll see at Semibayan

Coral reef and colorful seagrass in the same dive, in shallow water with good visibility. Cuttlefish, octopus and stingrays are regulars. Turtles, batfish and barracuda turn up too, though rarely, maybe one dive in ten. Once, between Semibayan and Rock Island, we met a dugong.

At night

After dark the shrimps, cuttlefish, squid, crabs, snails and moray eels come out, and the water sparks with bioluminescent plankton. Night dives are for Advanced divers, on request, with two divers or more.

Good to know

About three minutes by boat from Catangnan. Semibayan isn't a surf break, so it stays calm when the swell is big. A mooring line runs from the boat down to the reef, so going down and coming back up is easy. Any time of day works, and we dive here almost every day.

Who it suits

Every level, from a first Discover Scuba dive to rescue divers, divemasters and instructors. Open Water students do their ocean dives here and at Rock Island.

Dive Semibayan with us

A fun dive is two dives for ₱5,000 per person (private ₱8,500), with all gear, the boat and the marine fee included. You need an Open Water certification, and groups are five divers at most. Never dived? Start with Discover Scuba. Friends who don't dive can come along to snorkel. We choose the site for your level and the day, so ask us on WhatsApp what is possible on your dates.
