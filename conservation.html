Adopt-A-Mooring-Line

No anchors on the reef.

Boats here drop their anchors right on the coral. So we screw mooring lines into the sand, and the boats tie up to a red buoy instead.

Read the wikiDive with us

“The only way to truly protect something is to first fall in love with it.”

Why we started

Some corals at Rock Island and Stimpy's are older than our grandparents. We dive there almost every day, and we kept finding them broken by anchors. The corals can't protect themselves, so somebody has to.

Chie wrote to the local government about it back in 2018. In 2025 we stopped waiting and started with the people around us: boatmen, surfers, resorts and the barangay. Nobody here is the bad guy. Most people who drop an anchor have just never seen what's under it.

The mooring line wiki

Everything we learned, free for everyone.

We're not experts. We learned by doing it, and we made mistakes. Lines got stolen, some installs failed, some paint wouldn't stick. We wrote all of it down, so the next reef in the Philippines doesn't have to start from zero.
